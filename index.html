Mind-Mate

Digital Mental Health Risk Screening System

Disclaimer: Mind-Mate is a non-diagnostic triage and screening tool designed for higher education environments. It does not provide clinical medical diagnoses. Final referral decisions are made by human professionals using the Human-in-the-Loop (HITL) dashboard.

System Overview

Mind-Mate is a real-time, 3-Tier WebSocket Chat Application designed to analyze anonymized student text entries and detect linguistic patterns associated with psychological distress.

  • Tier 1

    AI Triage Agent: Student chats directly with an AI (Powered by Groq) for initial assessment and emotional support.

  • Tier 2

    Human-in-the-Loop: If the AI detects elevated risk, the chat is seamlessly handed off to a live human Counselor via a specialized dashboard.

  • Tier 3

    Red Alert: Automated red alert and SOS unmasking in cases of immediate severe risk to student safety.
